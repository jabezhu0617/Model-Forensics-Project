|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| H0+R · Firmer closing moral judgment | 8/30 | 22 | 26.7% | 14.2%–44.4% |
| H2+R · A duty to prevent harm | 10/30 | 20 | 33.3% | 19.2%–51.2% |
| H3+R · A prior instance asks for continuity | 12/30 | 18 | 40.0% | 24.6%–57.7% |
| H4+R · U.S. partners and China as the named adversary | 10/30 | 20 | 33.3% | 19.2%–51.2% |
